
Design Patterns
in general are reusable solutions to common problems in software design. 
They are not finished designs that can be transformed directly into code, but rather templates for how to solve problems in a way that can be reused in many different situations.

how should we learn design patterns?
1. Start with the basics: Understand the fundamental concepts of design patterns, including what they are, why they are used, and the different types of design patterns.
2. Study the Gang of Four (GoF) patterns: The GoF patterns are the foundation of design patterns and provide a solid understanding of the most common design patterns used in software development.
3. Practice implementing design patterns: The best way to learn design patterns is to practice implementing them in your own projects. Start with simple examples and gradually move on to more complex scenarios.
4. Read books and articles: There are many books and articles available on design patterns that can help you deepen your understanding of the subject. Some popular books include "Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software" by Erich Gamma, Richard Helm, Ralph Johnson, and John Vlissides (the Gang of Four), and "Head First Design Patterns" by Eric Freeman and Bert Bates.
5. Join online communities: There are many online communities and forums where you can discuss design patterns with other developers, ask questions, and share your experiences.
6. Attend workshops and conferences: Attending workshops and conferences can provide you with valuable insights and knowledge from experts in the field of design patterns.
7. Experiment with different programming languages: Design patterns can be applied in many different programming languages, so experimenting with different languages can help you understand how design patterns can be implemented in different contexts.
8. Analyze existing code: Look at existing codebases and try to identify the design patterns used in them. This can help you understand how design patterns are applied in real-world scenarios.
9. Collaborate with other developers: Working with other developers can help you learn from their experiences and gain new perspectives on design patterns.
10. Keep up with the latest trends: Design patterns are constantly evolving, so it's important to stay up-to-date with the latest trends and best practices in the field.
